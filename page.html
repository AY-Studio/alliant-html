Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026

1. Who We Are

Alliant AirFinance ("Alliant" or "we") is a global aviation finance platform focused on opportunities in commercial aviation. We combine an institutional investment strategy, deep relationships, and a hands-on approach to deliver attractive risk-adjusted returns for clients across market cycles. With teams across key aviation hubs, Alliant maintains a global footprint supported by professionals spanning finance, technical operations, legal, risk management, and underwriting.

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our practices, or wish to make a request regarding your personal information, please contact us through the channels listed on our website’s Contact page.

2. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Alliant AirFinance collects, uses, and discloses personal information, and explains the privacy rights available to you. It applies to information gathered both online (through this website and its pages, collectively the “Site”) and offline (through our business operations, events, or third-party sources).

By visiting the Site or interacting with us, you consent to the practices described here. We may update this Privacy Policy periodically, so please review it from time to time. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

Use of the Site is limited to individuals of legal age in their jurisdiction, and in all cases to individuals at least sixteen (16) years of age. If you are under 16, please do not use the Site or share any information.

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party websites, including the websites of our portfolio companies or partners, even if linked from the Site. When visiting other websites, please review their privacy policies.

3. Personal Information We Collect, Sources, and Purpose

The following describes the categories of personal information we may collect, the sources of that information, and the business purposes for which we use it.

3.1 Information We Do Not Collect

We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from minors under the age of eighteen (18).

3.2 Information You Provide Directly

You may voluntarily provide personal information (e.g., name, email, address, résumé, phone, investor status, or details about your inquiry) via our “Contact” forms or other communication channels (mail, email, phone, etc.). We keep records of the information and correspondence you provide to respond to inquiries, deliver customer service, and maintain business relationships.

If you wish to correct or update personal information you submitted, please contact us using the method described above.

3.3 Information from Third Parties During Due Diligence

We may receive personal information from third parties (such as companies in which we are considering investing) during due diligence. This information is shared confidentially and subject to nondisclosure agreements. We use it solely for the purpose of evaluating potential investments.

3.4 Information Automatically Collected via the Site

Like most websites, our Site automatically collects certain technical information during a visit, such as IP address, geolocation, browser type, operating system, and page usage history. We use this data to improve the Site, estimate usage patterns, enhance search results, and recognize returning users. We may also use the information to diagnose server issues, administer the Site, analyze trends, and gather aggregate statistics.

IP Address. Each time you visit the Site, we may automatically collect your IP address and the web page from which you arrived. IP addresses help us administer the Site, improve security, and understand aggregated user activity.

Cookies. When you visit the Site, we may place small data files (“cookies”) on your device to recognize you, store preferences, and enable certain functionality. Cookies are widely used to improve browsing experiences. You can adjust browser settings to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. However, disabling cookies may affect Site functionality or personalization.

Analytics. We may use analytics providers to help us understand usage patterns and improve the Site. These providers may collect information such as IP address, browser, pages viewed, and referring URLs. Analytics providers use this information solely to provide services to us and are contractually restricted from other uses.

4. How We Use Personal Information

We use personal information for the following business purposes:

  • Providing, operating, and improving our services and the Site;
  • Responding to inquiries and providing customer support;
  • Conducting due diligence, underwriting, and other investment activities;
  • Complying with legal obligations and enforcing our agreements;
  • Analyzing usage trends and enhancing user experience;
  • Protecting the security and integrity of our systems;
  • Executing marketing or business development initiatives (where permitted).

5. Disclosure of Personal Information

We may disclose personal information:

  • To affiliates, portfolio companies, or service providers that assist us in operating our business (subject to confidentiality obligations);
  • To professional advisors (e.g., lawyers, auditors) for legitimate business purposes;
  • As part of due diligence or transactional processes related to financing, mergers, or acquisitions;
  • In response to legal processes or governmental requests, or to comply with law;
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of Alliant, our clients, or others;
  • With your consent or at your direction.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data Security and Retention

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Although we strive to safeguard personal information, no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Access and obtain a copy of your personal information;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Request deletion or restriction of processing in certain circumstances;
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • Withdraw consent where we rely on consent to process data;
  • Opt out of marketing communications.

To exercise these rights, please contact us through the methods described on our Site. We will respond consistent with applicable law.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you may have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). These may include the right to know what categories of personal information we collect and disclose, the right to access or delete personal information, and the right to opt out of certain sharing. To submit a CCPA request, please use the privacy request form on our Site. We will confirm receipt within 10 days and respond within the timeframe required by law.

We are required to verify your identity (or your authorized representative’s identity) before responding to certain requests. Verification may include matching data points with information already maintained by Alliant. We will not use personal information provided for verification for any other purpose and will delete it when no longer needed for compliance.

9. International Data Transfers

Alliant operates globally. If you are located outside the United States, your personal information may be transferred to and processed in the United States or other jurisdictions that may not offer the same level of data protection. We take appropriate measures to protect personal information in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we make material changes, we will note the “Last Updated” date and post the revised policy on this page. Changes become effective when posted unless otherwise stated.

11. Contacting Alliant AirFinance

For questions about this Privacy Policy or to exercise your privacy rights, please reach us through the contact options provided on our Site. Please do not include sensitive personal information in messages sent through unsecured channels.
